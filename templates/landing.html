Engineered for Authentic In-Class Feedback

A closed 45-second window prevents link forwarding and guarantees real-time classroom feedback directly to the faculty member.

01

Faculty Initiates Session

At the conclusion of a topic, the teacher starts a session. The backend issues a unique scannable QR code and activates the 45-second timer.

02

Enrolled Students Check In

Students scan with their mobile phone or device. Identity is strictly verified from their university account. They answer 3 quick questions.

03

Instant Identified Results

At second zero, the session automatically closes. The teacher's screen transitions to show response analytics and the verified student roster.
